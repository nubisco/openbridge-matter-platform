## A second controller, not a second copy

Matter lets one device belong to several homes at once. OpenBridge joins your devices as an extra controller. Apple Home keeps controlling them, and nothing appears twice in the Home app.

## Local

OpenBridge reads each device directly over your network, with no cloud and no account. Thread devices are reached through the border routers you already have (a HomePod or an Apple TV).

## On the house plan

Lights, plugs, sensors, blinds and thermostats appear on the OpenBridge Devices page and the house plan, with history for temperature, humidity, power and battery.
